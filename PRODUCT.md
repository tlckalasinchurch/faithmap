# FaithMap — Kalasin House Mission Network

FaithMap is a mobile-first house mission group management, 3-step weekly reporting, pastoral care tracking, and Sunday 20:30 meeting intelligence platform built for the Kalasin church pilot network. It enables Group Leaders, Care Leaders/Coordinators, Body Leaders, Pastors, and System Administrators to submit weekly mission reports in under 3 minutes, verify and unlock reports, monitor 18-district Kalasin geographic progress, track member baptism/discipleship journeys and confidential pastoral alerts, and govern multi-role group responsibilities.

## Platform

web

## Users & Context

- **Primary Users:** Next-generation Gen Z and Gen Alpha youth leaders, house group leaders, care coordinators, body leaders, and pastors across Kalasin province (`กาฬสินธุ์`).
- **Context of Use:** Used primarily on smartphones right after weekly house group fellowships and during the Sunday 20:30 provincial leadership huddle, as well as on desktop/tablet for regional oversight and administration.
- **Core Jobs to Be Done:**
  1. Submit 3-step weekly house group mission reports (Attendance, Discipleship & Giving, Prayer & Pastoral Care Signals) in under 3 minutes with offline draft resilience.
  2. Review, approve, or unlock submitted reports with clear status visibility while keeping draft content private to the author (`FR-07.5`).
  3. Generate an instant Sunday 20:30 Meeting Brief with KPI deltas, missing-report callouts, and one-tap copy for team chat.
  4. Explore the 18-district Kalasin mission map with coordinate privacy protection (`DEC-06` masked coordinates for non-privileged roles).
  5. Track member spiritual milestones (`A1`–`A3`), newcomer retention, and confidential pastoral care alerts with PDPA consent enforcement (`DEC-10`).
  6. Manage flexible group responsibility assignments (`LEADER`, `CARE_HOLDER`, `DELEGATE_REPORTER`, `CO_LEADER`) and historical period reassignments (`CD-01`–`CD-10`).

## Voice, Tone & Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual World:** **Kinetic Pop Bento & Neo-Brutalist Tactile** (Gen Z / Gen Alpha Club & Creator Studio energy).
- **Personality:** Energetic, tactile, fearless, high-craft, joyful, and unmistakably modern — rejecting clinical hospital teal, stiff corporate ERP tables, and bureaucratic form layouts.
- **Material & Surface Language:** Crisp dark-ink structural borders (`2px solid #141413`), tactile offset hard shadows (`4px 4px 0px #141413`) that physically depress on click/tap, modular Bento grid cards, bold pill stickers/badges, and high-contrast warm paper/canvas grounds paired with Electric Acid Lime (`#CCFF00`), Cyber Violet (`#6D28D9` / `#7C3AED`), Punch Coral (`#FF4D6D`), and Solar Amber (`#FFB703`) accents.
- **Non-Negotiable Ergonomics:** Minimum `48px` touch targets, `>= 16px` Thai body typography with generous line-height for effortless Thai vowel/tone-mark legibility, WCAG AA contrast across every badge and button, and zero Web Push dependencies (`DEC-07`).
